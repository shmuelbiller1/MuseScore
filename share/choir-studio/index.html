Personal Voice Choir Studio experimental · offline

Use your own recording and a MIDI export of the same MuseScore score. Map its four distinct note tracks below. MIDI carries the score's performed tempo, rhythms, rests, note lengths and velocities; the recording supplies only timbre. This is an offline vowel-based proof of concept, not realistic singing or lyric/consonant synthesis. A wide pitch shift may sound unnatural.

Assign a different score track to each part. Each may use the same recording, but the written notes are never copied between parts. Pitch correction at 100% keeps the written note; lower values intentionally relax that guarantee.
